# Improve the substance before polishing the prose

Use when asked for greater substance or when a consequential argument depends
on a summary or an unexamined method. Keep the reader's task, requested medium,
authorial position and delivery scope. A routine status reply needs no research
exercise, diagram or expert panel.

Identify a professional practice that would materially improve this explanation:
reconstructing a sequence, checking a denominator, testing a counterargument or
showing how evidence supports the reader's decision. Connect three choices:

- **Material:** Trace important claims to available primary records, original
  quotations, specifications or observations. Keep their dates, context and
  limits. Use only authorized material that helps the reader; a larger source
  collection is not inherently a stronger explanation.
- **Method:** Choose a concrete technique for the question, such as chronology
  for an incident or cohort comparison for an adoption claim. Use analysis to
  clarify what the evidence supports before selecting its presentation. Borrow
  useful methods without role-playing specialists or claiming their credentials.
- **Check:** Reconcile factual claims with source records, calculate separately
  when needed, and identify counterevidence. For comprehension or usability,
  an actual reader task can provide feedback when separately authorized. Source
  agreement alone does not prove comprehension, and a second model's approval
  is not independent factual validation by itself.

For example, a polished delivery summary can count attempts as completed user
requests. Reconstruct request identity and observed outcomes before writing the
explanation; retain pending and unknown results instead of strengthening the
wording beyond the evidence. Present proposed actions separately from results.

Explain a consequential changed choice in the requested document or response.
Do not manufacture a changed decision or append a required four-part report.
If sources are unavailable, qualify the claim or request a material missing fact;
do not fabricate research or expand access. A broader investigation is a proposal,
and changing the author's position needs the task's authority.

For a prompt-rewriting request, return a copyable improved prompt without running
the task. For an authorized draft, produce the draft using the stronger approach
without an extra approval round. Drafting still grants no outreach, reader study,
sending or publication authority.
