# Precise technical writing

Use this optional profile for operational instructions or reference prose where
ambiguity impedes a concrete action. It is inspired by Simplified Technical
English principles; it is not the ASD-STE100 standard, a controlled vocabulary,
or a compliance assessment. Preserve consumer style and the domain's necessary
terms. General explanations and ordinary conversation need not use this profile.

## Clarify the action and its conditions

Name the actor or component when responsibility could be ambiguous. Use a
concrete verb and identify its object. In instructions, separate actions when
they have different prerequisites, outcomes or recovery paths. Keep a necessary
condition next to the action it governs; separate prose should not hide an
exception required for correct execution.

Order steps by actual dependencies. State the observable success condition and
the supported next action when an expected result is absent. Preserve supplied
commands, identifiers, units and technical meanings exactly. Do not simplify an
instruction so far that it changes the permitted operation.

## Make meaning consistent

Use one established term for the same concept and explain a necessary unfamiliar
term at its first meaningful use. Resolve pronouns with several possible
referents. Prefer a direct sentence to chains of abstractions or verbs that
conceal who acts. Shorter sentences help when they remove ambiguity; word counts
are not a substitute for preserving the full condition or causal explanation.

Keep observed behavior, required behavior and proposed changes distinct. Retain
uncertainty, factual qualifiers, evidence links and the original scope of a
claim. A clear sentence with an unsupported fact still needs correction or a
visible evidence gap.

## Check the result

Walk through the procedure or lookup task using the written text and supplied
facts. Look for an action with no named target, a condition applied to the wrong
step, an unexplained term, or a success claim the reader cannot observe. Use
actual verification when authorized and available; a prose walkthrough alone
does not prove a command or product behavior works.

Return editable prose in the requested format. Describe any material ambiguity
that remains. Do not claim formal standard compliance or require a universal
approved-word list from this profile.
