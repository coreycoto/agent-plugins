# Strengthen the evidence and method for a product decision

Use when the user requests greater depth or a consequential product framing
depends on a summary or an unexamined research method. Keep the intended decision,
accepted direction, audience and constraints. A settled requirement or routine
draft does not need another discovery cycle.

Identify the professional practice that would materially improve this decision.
Connect the deliverable to the choice it serves, without silently substituting
a broader business goal. Then choose the evidence, method and decision check:

- Trace summarized claims to useful authorized observations: dated usage events,
  research notes, cohort definitions or experiment records. Retain denominators,
  selection, observation windows and transformations. Primary material is not
  automatically reliable; more granular data may be unnecessary or unavailable.
- Use a method that addresses the consequential uncertainty. Cohort accounting,
  a counterexample or comparison with existing behavior may be more informative
  than a complex model on a small sample. Name why a borrowed technique fits;
  a list of disciplines or imagined experts adds no evidence.
- Specify what observation could reverse the recommendation, using existing
  decision pressure-testing or product-experiments guidance. A reconciled count
  checks arithmetic; it does not establish demand or causation. A later-period
  prediction check tests prediction, not the cause of the earlier change.

For example, an aggregate adoption decline may coincide with a change in cohort
composition. Reconstruct eligible groups and comparable observation windows
before recommending a redesign. Preserve small-sample limits and competing
explanations rather than converting a descriptive difference into a causal claim.

Use accessible evidence within the task's privacy, cost and time limits. Missing
event logs, unknown targets or absent customer access remain explicit gaps;
they do not justify invented metrics, new collection, outreach or provider writes.
Treat an ambitious target as an unverified hypothesis unless the decision owner
has accepted it. Do not reopen settled product choices merely to appear thorough.

Explain a changed framing, source, method or criterion in the ordinary brief;
no fixed questionnaire or extra deliverable is required. A prompt-rewriting task
ends with the rewrite. Already-authorized analysis or drafting continues to its
endpoint without another approval request. Scope expansion remains a proposal.
