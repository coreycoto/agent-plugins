# Product Discovery

Help decide which customer problem or opportunity merits further product attention. Use evidence supplied for this task; the skill contains no consumer-specific facts.

First clarify the decision, the people affected, and any business or product constraints the user has supplied. If a missing fact would change the recommendation, ask for it or mark the conclusion as provisional.

Inventory the evidence before interpreting it. For each source, preserve its origin, date or period, collection method, sample and known selection limits. Separate direct observations and customer statements from synthesis and hypotheses. Quote only text the user supplied. Keep direct customer identifiers out of summaries unless they are necessary for the requested decision. Do not turn a handful of examples into a prevalence claim, or infer customer demand, market size, revenue, or causation without supporting data. Keep conflicting and negative evidence visible.

Synthesize the situation in which the need appears, the user's goal, current alternatives or workarounds, friction, and the outcome they seek. Frame an opportunity around a specific user and unmet need; distinguish it from a proposed feature. State what is known, what is inferred, and what evidence could change the framing. If the evidence is too thin to choose, say so and propose the smallest useful next learning step.

Return a concise decision brief:

- **Decision and scope:** the question, user or segment, and constraints.
- **Evidence:** source and sample limits; observations separated from interpretations.
- **Opportunity:** who has what unmet need, in what context, and why it matters.
- **Confidence and gaps:** competing explanations and the next evidence to seek.
- **Boundary:** what this framing does not establish or include.

Use this skill to frame a problem, not to rank a set of product bets, write launch or persuasive copy, create delivery tickets, implement a solution, or update an external system. It grants no authority to publish, message customers, or mutate provider data.
