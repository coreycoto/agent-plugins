We are considering automatic weekly emailed exports.
Eight of ten interviewed admins liked the idea; recruitment targeted the most active accounts.
Over four weeks, 42 accounts were eligible, seven exported at least once, and two exported in more than one week.
Reasons for nonuse were not collected. One customer has a contractual weekly report requirement and exports manually.
Email infrastructure, delivery cost, staffing and numeric go/no-go thresholds are not established.
